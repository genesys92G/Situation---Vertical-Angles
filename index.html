Answer

Vertically opposite angles

Angles directly opposite each other always have the same measure.

What does that mean? When two lines cross, opposite angles (the ones facing each other) are equal.

Rule

Opposite angles are equal.

∠1 = ∠3   and   ∠2 = ∠4

Conclusion: all four cannot be different—each angle has an opposite angle with the same measure.
